Chore Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Chore Quest is a family chore and reward application designed to help parents and families organize chores, quests, rewards, badges, and progress.

Information Stored by Chore Quest

Chore Quest may store information entered by a parent or authorized adult for the purpose of providing the app's features. This may include:

  • Child names and optional profile pictures or avatars
  • Optional child PIN information
  • Quest and chore assignments
  • Quest completion records
  • XP, levels, streaks, and other progress information
  • Badges and achievements
  • Rewards and reward redemptions
  • Family quest, reward, badge, and configuration information
  • Notification preferences and related notification information

How Information Is Used

Information stored by Chore Quest is used to provide the app's core functionality, including displaying quests, tracking progress, awarding XP and badges, managing rewards, and maintaining family settings.

Where Information Is Stored

Chore Quest is designed to store family information locally on the user's device using Apple's SwiftData and related local storage technologies.

Chore Quest does not operate a Chore Quest cloud account or hosted family database.

Parent PIN Security

The parent PIN is stored using Apple's Keychain services rather than being stored as ordinary application data.

Notifications

Chore Quest may use Apple's local notification services to provide notifications configured by the family. Chore Quest does not use notifications for advertising.

Advertising and Analytics

Chore Quest does not contain advertising networks and does not use third-party analytics SDKs for behavioral advertising or tracking.

Information Chore Quest Does Not Intentionally Collect

Chore Quest does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, or behavioral advertising profiles.

Children's Information

Child-related information in Chore Quest is entered and managed by a parent or authorized adult for use within the family application. Parents and authorized adults should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into the app.

Sharing of Information

Chore Quest does not sell or rent family information and does not use family information for advertising purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Family information remains on the device until it is removed by the user or the application is deleted. Deleting the Chore Quest application removes its local application data subject to the behavior of the operating system and any device backups.

Third-Party Services

Chore Quest relies on Apple platform services for functionality such as secure credential storage and local notifications. Chore Quest does not operate a separate hosted database or advertising platform.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Chore Quest's functionality or privacy practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
